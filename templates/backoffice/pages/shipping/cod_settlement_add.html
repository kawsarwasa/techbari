How this posts

Select shipments from one courier only. Each selected amount creates a Sale Payment for that order and immediately marks that transaction Reconciled under this courier settlement.

Gross COD is used for customer payment settlement. Courier deduction is stored separately so the later Accounting module can post the fee/expense correctly. Net received = Gross COD − Courier Deduction.
