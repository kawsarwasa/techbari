Shipment Information

Fulfillment Rules

COD Expected is automatic. It always uses the selected Sales Order's current outstanding balance. A fully paid order will show ৳0 and does not require courier COD collection.

Customer Shipping Charge is revenue charged to the customer. Courier Fee is what the courier charges your business, so the two amounts may be different.

Tracking ID and Courier Reference are optional and are entered manually when the courier provides them. They can later be automated through courier API integration.

Creating a shipment does not deduct inventory. Inventory is issued only when the parcel is handed to the courier.

One fulfillment shipment is currently kept per order so the existing Sales inventory issue remains atomic and consistent.
